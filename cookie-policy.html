Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how PurePath ("we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on purepath.digital (the "Site"). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, such as pixels and local storage, can store or read information on your device in comparable ways. We refer to all of these together as "cookies" in this policy.

How we use cookies

We use a limited set of cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your cookie choice so we do not ask you again on every visit.
  • Understand how visitors reach and use the Site, so we can measure interest and improve it.
  • Measure the performance of advertising campaigns that bring visitors to the Site.

Advertising and analytics

When you accept cookies, we may load a tracking pixel from X (formerly Twitter) that helps us understand which visits and sign-ups came from our advertising. This allows us to measure campaign performance. If you decline, we do not load this advertising pixel.

Your choices

When you first visit the Site, a banner lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, which will make the banner appear again on your next visit.

Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the Site work.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above.
